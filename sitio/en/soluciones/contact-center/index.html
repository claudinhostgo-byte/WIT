Calls through your usual carrier

The customer dials the number they already know. The call comes in through the SIP trunks of your current carrier, whether Entel, Movistar, Claro, WOM, GTD or another: you keep your numbers and your contract.

SIP trunksYour numbersAny carrier

WhatsApp Business messages

Built for Meta Business accounts: messages arrive from the official WhatsApp Business Platform API. The account, the number and the verification in Meta Business Manager remain yours.

WhatsApp Business PlatformMeta Business Manager

Instagram direct messages

For Instagram professional accounts linked to your Meta Business Manager. Direct messages come into the same desktop as WhatsApp and calls. Instagram is not a native Dynamics 365 channel: that is why W-IT Bridge exists.

Instagram professional accountMeta Business Manager

Web chat and email

They come in through the native Dynamics 365 channels and share the same customer history as calls and messages.

Web chatEmail

Voice option 1: AudioCodes SBC

An AudioCodes SBC, certified for Microsoft Teams, connects your carrier to Teams Phone through Direct Routing. It can be physical or virtual on Azure. W-IT designs the voice architecture, configures it with the carrier and tests quality and load.

AudioCodes SBCMicrosoft Teams PhoneDirect Routing

Voice option 2: your own SBC

If your company already runs an SBC certified for Microsoft Teams, we connect it to Teams Phone through Direct Routing and you do not need to buy another one. This is the typical case for carriers and telecom companies.

Teams-certified SBCDirect Routing

Messaging option 1: W-IT Bridge

Developed by W-IT for Meta Business accounts. It receives every WhatsApp and Instagram message directly from Meta's APIs, identifies the customer in the CRM and delivers it to the right queue. It does not store conversations and runs in W-IT's cloud or in yours.

W-IT BridgeMeta Business accountsInstagram included

Learn about W-IT Bridge →

Messaging option 2: Twilio or another provider

WhatsApp can also come in through a messaging provider (BSP) such as Twilio, using the Dynamics 365 WhatsApp channel. W-IT Bridge is the alternative when you want a direct connection to Meta, Instagram included and rule-based routing.

BSPDynamics 365 WhatsApp channel

Platform: queues and skills per channel

Each channel has its own queues, skills and rules: IVR and recording for voice; templates and business hours for WhatsApp and Instagram; SLAs and cases for chat and email. Unified routing assigns every contact by skill, priority and team capacity, and supervisors see everything in real time.

Queues per channelSkillsUnified routingReal-time supervision

AI agent: answers first, in voice and chat

A Copilot Studio agent answers common questions at any hour, queries your systems and resolves what it can. When a person is needed, it hands off to the human agent with the full context of the conversation.

Copilot StudioConversational IVRHandoff with context

The agent receives the handoff with context

They handle calls and messages on a single screen, with a summary of what the AI agent already discussed, the customer history and the Dynamics 365 Customer Service cases and SLAs. Copilot suggests replies and closes the case.

How we do it

Success by Design, in waves.

Microsoft's implementation framework for Dynamics 365, delivered in short waves: the first goes live with the most urgent process and the next ones extend what is already in use.

  1. We understand your business and define what goes live first.

    Success by Design · Solution Blueprint Review begins

    What we do

    • Requirements gathering and validation with process owners.
    • Mapping of current processes, data and systems.
    • Wave definition: what goes live first and what comes next.
    • Prioritization of AI use cases and assessment of data and governance.

    What you get

    • High-level solution approach.
    • Wave plan with scope and success criteria.
    • Risks identified from the start.

    AIAI in this phase

    Automated analysis of environments and the entity-relationship model, and prioritization of AI use cases.

  2. We design the complete solution before building.

    Success by Design · Solution Blueprint Review

    What we do

    • Definition of every workstream and the detailed plan.
    • Architecture: data model, security, integrations and ALM.
    • Scope of each agent and human oversight points.
    • Responsible AI review: privacy, transparency and accountability.

    What you get

    • Solution Blueprint: the complete solution design.
    • Updated project plan.
    • Testing and data migration strategies.

    AIAI in this phase

    Design and business rule drafts generated with AI and reviewed by our architects.

  3. We build in sprints, with demos and technical reviews along the way.

    Success by Design · Implementation Reviews

    What we do

    • Configuration and development in sprints, with a demo at the end of each one.
    • Integrations and data migration.
    • Implementation Reviews: data, security, integration, ALM and testing.
    • Agent prototyping and evaluation: accuracy, coverage, safety and latency.

    What you get

    • Tested functional increments every sprint.
    • Findings and recommendations from each review.
    • Agents evaluated with real scenarios.

    AIAI in this phase

    AI-assisted customizations and automated testing.

  4. We prepare the people and the go-live.

    Success by Design · Go-live Readiness Review

    What we do

    • User acceptance testing (UAT) and user training.
    • Cutover plan with go/no-go criteria and go-live rehearsals.
    • Support model and deployment runbook.
    • Agent validation with representative production data and monitoring in place.

    What you get

    • Trained users and approved UAT.
    • Cutover plan and deployment runbook.
    • Go-live with reinforced support (hypercare).

    AIAI in this phase

    Real usage measured from the start to guide adoption.

  5. We stabilize, measure and prepare the next wave.

    Success by Design · Stabilization and transition to support

    What we do

    • Post-go-live stabilization.
    • Application management services (AMS).
    • Continuous agent monitoring: response quality, usage and drift.
    • Next wave: improvements and new use cases.

    What you get

    • AMS support with agreed service levels.
    • Periodic agent evaluations and platform updates.
    • Solution evolution roadmap.

    AIAI in this phase

    Agents in production with continuous monitoring and periodic evaluations.
