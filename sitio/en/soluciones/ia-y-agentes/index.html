How we do it

From prototype to production, with continuous evaluation.

Prototype with real data, measurable evaluation before deployment and permanent monitoring. Based on the Cloud Adoption Framework's agent adoption guidance, Success by Design's AI-first practices and Microsoft Foundry observability.

  1. We choose the use case by its value, the available data and the risk.

    Milestone · Use case and metrics approved

    What we do

    • Use case workshop: prioritization by value, feasibility and risk.
    • AI maturity level and adoption barriers: from assisted to autonomous.
    • Agent type: productivity, action or automation.
    • Available data and knowledge: quality, permissions and gaps.

    What you get

    • Prioritized use cases with success metrics.
    • Agent definition: scope, channel and degree of autonomy.
    • Data and permissions plan.

    AIResponsible AI

    Every use case is assessed for risk: what the agent may decide and what stays in human hands.

  2. Rules before code: responsible AI, security and environment ready.

    Milestone · Responsible AI review

    What we do

    • Responsible AI principles applied to the case: transparency, privacy and fairness.
    • Agent scope, limits and escalation paths to people.
    • Least-privilege access to data and systems, with auditing.
    • Environment prepared: Copilot Studio or Azure AI Foundry, connectors and channels.

    What you get

    • Agent usage and governance policy.
    • Human oversight and escalation design.
    • Environment ready to build.

    AIResponsible AI

    Every automated decision has an owner, a record and a person who can step in.

  3. Prototype with real data and an evaluation set from the start.

    Milestone · Evaluation passed

    What we do

    • Agent prototype: instructions, knowledge, tools and memory.
    • Evaluation set with real scenarios; quality and safety evaluators.
    • Short iterations: measure, adjust and measure again.
    • Adversarial testing (red teaming) before exposing it to users.

    What you get

    • Working agent tested with pilot users.
    • Evaluation results: accuracy, groundedness, safety and latency.
    • Design decision log.

    AIResponsible AI

    No version moves forward without passing the quality and safety evaluations.

  4. We go live by channel and group, with monitoring from the first minute.

    Milestone · Go-live with responsible AI approval

    What we do

    • Gradual rollout: pilot, expansion and opening by channel (Teams, web, WhatsApp).
    • Integration with the systems and the real workflow.
    • Telemetry, traces and alerts active before go-live.
    • Training for users and the support team.

    What you get

    • Agent in production with monitoring.
    • Usage, quality and cost dashboard.
    • Incident response plan.

    AIResponsible AI

    Documented approval from business owners before going live.

  5. We measure quality and value continuously, and expand the cases that deliver results.

    Milestone · Periodic quality and value review

    What we do

    • Continuous evaluation on real traffic and scheduled tests.
    • Drift monitoring after model updates.
    • Tuning of instructions, knowledge and tools based on usage.
    • New use cases and greater autonomy when the data supports it.

    What you get

    • Periodic quality, usage and cost report.
    • Backlog of improvements and new agents.
    • Reviewed autonomy level.

    AIResponsible AI

    People keep supervising: they review samples, escalated cases and drift.
